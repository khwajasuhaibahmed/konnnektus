CLIENT School of Leadership (SoL)
Event Coverage Social Media PR

Lead Forward Sindh — Youth Volunteerism Camp (Karachi Division)

Lead Forward Sindh – Youth Volunteerism Camp (Karachi Division) brought together 100 young volunteers from across the city for a powerful day of reflection, learning, and action centered on the true spirit of service.

Under the vision of Honorable Minister Sardar Muhammad Bux Khan Mahar, this initiative by the Sports and Youth Affairs Department, Government of Sindh in collaboration with School of Leadership helped youth see volunteerism as leadership in action and translate their intent to serve into practical community impact.

With over 500 applicants, 100 committed changemakers were selected for this experiential journey. Through dialogue, shared stories, and structured reflection, participants explored how meaningful change begins with individuals who choose to step forward for others. We were honored by the presence of Chief Guest Munawar Ali Mahesar, Secretary, Sports & Youth Affairs Department, Government of Sindh. We also thank Hammad Tanveer, Chairman, Hammad Foundation, for briefly sharing his journey of service and purpose.

This is only the beginning. Lead Forward Sindh will continue across more divisions, creating more platforms for youth to come together, learn, and lead through volunteerism. Stay tuned!
